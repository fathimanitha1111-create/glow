Cleansing Advice

Cleanser Questions Answered

How to cleanse gently without drying out reactive skin.

Do soap-free face washes really remove waterproof makeup and sunscreen?
Yes! For waterproof mascara or heavy SPF, we recommend our Kind Micellar Cleansing Water on a soft cotton pad first, followed by our Refreshing Facial Gel Wash for a complete, double-cleansed clean without scrubbing.
How often should I wash my face if I have sensitive or dry skin?
Dermatologists advise cleansing twice daily: in the morning to wash away nighttime sebum and cellular turnover, and at night to remove city pollutants and sunscreen. If your skin is extremely dry, rinse with lukewarm water in the morning and use our Gel Wash in the evening.
Will this cleanser sting my eyes?
No. Our Refreshing Facial Gel Wash and Micellar Cleansing Water are ophthalmologist tested and specially formulated with zero perfumes or harsh surfactants, making them safe for sensitive eyes and contact lens wearers.
