Kind Skincare Questions

Everything you need to know about our sensitive skin formulations.

Are all products really 100% free of artificial perfumes?
Yes, absolutely. Fragrance is the #1 cause of allergic contact dermatitis in cosmetics. None of our products contain synthetic fragrances, masking perfumes, or essential oils that trigger sensitivity.
Can I use these products if I have eczema or rosacea?
Yes! All our formulas are dermatologically tested, non-comedogenic, and hypoallergenic. Our Calming Cica Drops and Replenishing Rich Moisturizer are specifically favored by those dealing with barrier damage, eczema-prone patches, and rosacea.
What is your shipping and return policy?
We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $40. If any product causes an adverse reaction or doesn't suit your sensitive skin, we offer a 30-day "Kind Skin Guarantee" full refund.
