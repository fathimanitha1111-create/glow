How to Layer Active Serums Safely

When dealing with sensitive skin, layering correctly prevents overload. Follow our golden rule: thinnest water-based consistency first, followed by richer treatments:

01 Morning: Cleanse → 2 drops Hyaluronic Acid → Vitamin C → Light Moisturizer.
02 Evening: Cleanse → 2-3 drops 10% Niacinamide → Cica Barrier Drops → Rich Moisturizer.
03 Always apply serums on slightly damp skin to maximize absorption.

Serum Questions Answered

How to get maximum active performance with minimal irritation.

Can I use 10% Niacinamide if my skin is very sensitive?
Yes. Niacinamide (Vitamin B3) is actually a barrier-repairing agent that strengthens lipid synthesis and calms redness. Unlike pure retinoids or glycolic acid, it does not thin the skin or cause peeling.
Why does traditional Vitamin C irritate my skin, but not Glow & Care?
Many traditional formulas use L-Ascorbic Acid formulated at a harsh, stinging pH of 2.5 to 3.0. Glow & Care uses stabilized 3-O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid buffered at skin-comfortable pH 5.5, delivering identical brightening without the tingling redness.
How many drops should I use per application?
Just 2 to 3 drops are sufficient for the entire face and neck. Gently press into the skin with the palms of your hands rather than rubbing vigorously.
