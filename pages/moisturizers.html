Common Moisturizer Questions

Tips on application, layering, and keeping pores clear.

Will these moisturizers cause clogged pores or breakouts?
No. All Glow & Care moisturizers are non-comedogenic and dermatologically evaluated. We omit heavy pore-clogging waxes and mineral oils, using skin-compatible plant glycerin and borage seed oil instead.
Can I apply sunscreen and makeup over these formulas?
Yes! Both the Hydrating Light Moisturizer and Water Boost Gel create a smooth, velvety canvas that allows makeup and sunscreen to glide on without pilling or rolling.
What does Pro-Vitamin B5 actually do for the skin?
Also known as Panthenol, Pro-Vitamin B5 is a proven humectant that attracts water to deep epidermis layers while boosting tissue regeneration and soothing erythema (redness).
