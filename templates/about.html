The Kind Skincare Philosophy

Skincare that treats your sensitive skin with kindness

We believe that clean, healthy skin should never come at the cost of irritation, tightness, or allergic flare-ups. What we leave out of our products is just as important as what we put in.

Our Origin

Inspired by the belief that gentle is powerful

In a beauty industry crowded with harsh 20% acid peels, heavily perfumed creams, and overwhelming 12-step rituals, Glow & Care was founded to restore simplicity and peace of mind.

Over 70% of people report having sensitive skin prone to redness, eczema, dry patches, or stinging. Yet most conventional cosmetics still include synthetic dyes, overpowering perfumes, and harsh detergent sulfates.

"Kindness is not just a marketing word for us. It is the core scientific principle guiding every single molecule we formulate."

We prioritize your biological skin barrier. By matching the skin's natural acidic pH of 5.5 and selecting only dermatologically proven ingredients like Pro-Vitamin B5 and triple purified water, we give your complexion everything it needs to flourish.

Ingredient Integrity

The Zero List: What We Strictly Leave Out

Common cosmetic additives that trigger sensitivity have zero place in our formulas.

🚫

0% Artificial Perfumes

Synthetic fragrances are the #1 cause of contact dermatitis and redness. All our products are 100% unscented.

🧼

0% Harsh Soaps & Sulfates

No SLS or SLES. Our cleansing agents gently remove dirt while leaving your essential lipid barrier untouched.

🧪

0% Parabens & Phthalates

We use gentle, food-grade preservatives that keep products fresh without upsetting endocrine balance.

🍸

0% Drying Alcohols

Denatured alcohol strips hydration and causes premature aging. We formulate with zero drying alcohols.

🎨

0% Synthetic Colors & Dyes

Artificial coloring agents add no benefit to skin health. Our formulas are naturally clear or soft milky white.

🐰

0% Animal Testing

Certified cruelty-free by PETA. We never test on animals, nor do we sell in markets that require animal testing.

Formulation Standard

Pillar 03

Dermatologist Evaluated

Hypoallergenic and non-comedogenic. Clinically tested on sensitive, reactive, and contact-lens wearing individuals.

Pillar 04

Optimal pH 5.5 Balance

Precisely calibrated to mirror the human acid mantle, preventing bacterial colonization while maintaining ideal moisture levels.
